*Effective November 22, 2026*

Angela Writes! is software for a personal writing device. This policy
describes what it does with your information when you use its Google Drive
sync.

## In short

Angela Writes! does not collect, store, sell or share your personal
information. It has no server: your documents go directly between your device
and your own Google Drive, and the developer never receives them.

## What the app can access

If you choose to sign in to Google Drive on the device, it asks Google for one
permission: to see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive
files it creates (the `drive.file` scope). It cannot see other files in your
Drive, and it does not ask for your email address, contacts or profile.

## How that access is used

- Documents you write on the device are saved as Google Docs in a folder named
  "Angela Writes" in your Google Drive.
- Changes you make to those Docs are copied back to the device.
- Documents you delete on the device are moved to your Google Drive trash, and
  Docs you delete from that folder are deleted from the device.

The access is used for nothing else: no advertising, analytics or profiling.

## Where your information is kept

- Your documents are kept on the device's SD card and in your Google Drive.
- The sign-in token that lets the device reach your Drive is kept in the
  device's internal memory, and nowhere else.
- Google stores the Docs in your Drive under
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party other than Google.

## USB sync

The device can also sync documents with a folder on your computer over a USB
cable. Those documents stay on your device and your computer.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Angela Writes!'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs
adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

- Sign out of Google Drive on the device from Menu > Sync.
- Remove the device's access at any time at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- Delete the Docs, or the whole "Angela Writes" folder, in Google Drive.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
